Ollie's Chess Check-Up

Hi! I'm Ollie. Let's see how much chess you know! There are 60 puzzles. They start easy and get super tricky, even for grown-ups. If a puzzle is too hard, tap Skip. That's OK!

For grown-ups

This is the online version of the printable Check-Up. The child answers on the screen; when they tap Finish, the page works out a level from 1 to 9 and an estimated rating from 100 to 1800+ (the chess.com scale), with a review of every puzzle.

Young children: one section per sitting works well, and the page remembers where they stopped. Read questions aloud if needed (or turn on 🔊), but please give no hints. Skipped puzzles count as not solved, and nothing is marked right or wrong until the end.

Answers are saved only in this browser on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere. The printable booklet, the guide and the scoring rules are on the Check-Up page.
